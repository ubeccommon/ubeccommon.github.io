*The Erdpuls pattern card, adapted for this project under the open
hamlet paper, Section 6.3. A card stays in the workshop for good; the
yearly narrative draws on it, and only the narrative is published.*

*No child's name appears on a card. Discoveries made in a session are
attributed to the cohort. Place is recorded at ring level only, never
by coordinates. Knowledge that belongs to the elders or to a household
enters only with their consent, on their terms.*

* * *
