*This document states a general advocacy method, beyond any
single instance of it. That is a large claim, and the claim
carries its own limit, stated in §8: history supplies
converged instances of the combination; what remains unproven
is whether design can reproduce what constraint discovered.
The method has no proper name; it is called here, for lack of
one, a trimtab method — see §9. This document was developed
with assistance from Claude (Anthropic PBC). All strategic
decisions and commitments are those of the author.*

* * *

## 1. The diagnosis — what existing methods share

A survey of the advocacy field (rebuttal and fact-checking,
framing and narrative change, deep canvassing, solutions
journalism, social-norms messaging, moral reframing,
hope-based communications, entertainment-education,
crisis-mapping, twinning and sponsorship) finds four
properties shared across nearly all of it:

- **Episodic.** The unit of work is the burst — one
  conversation, one article, one exposure, one campaign. Few
  methods use time itself as the evidence; the ones that did
  (§8) were not designed to, and are the exception the record
  supplies.
- **Extractive.** A story is taken from a subject and carried
  to an audience. The subject is material; ethics arrives
  afterward as compliance, not as architecture.
- **Persuasive.** Even the gentlest modes lead with a vision
  they want adopted, or aim at the audience's values. The ask
  is present, however softly it is held.
- **Unfalsifiable.** Almost no method states in advance what
  its own failure looks like. Deep canvassing is the honest
  exception — it measured, and published its nulls.

Each property is a reasonable adaptation to campaign
economics. Together they produce the landscape audiences have
learned to tune out.

## 2. The method — four commitments

**Duration as evidence.** The unit of proof is the return, not
the claim. The method publishes only what can be revisited,
and schedules the revisit into the form itself. The same
subject, seen again, further grown — duration is the one thing
hostile propaganda cannot counterfeit, because a lie must be
maintained and a garden only watered. Where other methods
argue, this one waits in public.

**Membership, not extraction.** The person shown owns the
showing. Consent names the reach and the permanence edge
before anything publishes; belonging attaches to the
contribution, and withdrawal remains possible along the whole
trace gradient. This is structure, not compliance — the shown
person is a member of the thing, never its content.

**Demonstration, not persuasion.** No narrator, no ask, no
concluding caption. The audience completes the meaning or
nothing happens. On-ramps exist — follow, twin, fund, visit —
but every on-ramp is a door, never a hand on the back. The
only conversion that counts is the step the viewer takes as
their own.

**Falsifiability in the room.** The method states its
predicted results in advance, including expected near-nulls on
single exposure; it carries a hostile-read round that can
remove whole content classes before they are built; it names
its own failure conditions (§7). A method that cannot say what
would disprove it is a belief.

The four commitments are load-bearing together. Any three
without the fourth collapse into something the field already
has: duration without membership is surveillance; membership
without demonstration is a co-op newsletter; demonstration
without falsifiability is faith.

**Three design rules** the commitments imply but do not state.
They come from a design model beneath the method — tuning
rather than transmission, set out with its transfer ledger in
the advocacy reference's §7 and observed at length in the
companion case history on resonance — and they are stated here
in the method's own terms, without that model's vocabulary:

1. **Choose selectivity, and say which.** Depth of response
   and breadth of audience trade off; a narrow, well-defined
   audience with a deep accumulating response is a legitimate
   and usually superior choice to broad reach with fast decay.
   Carriers before crowds is the narrow choice. The choice is
   named per instance, because it also chooses what is
   measured.

2. **One drop point, in sequence.** Inputs out of phase with
   each other cancel, so the entry point is one and it is
   chosen: one point, one rhythm, proven or falsified, then
   the next. Criteria: a rhythm already running; a carrier
   whose own audience follows them; low damping — the moveable
   middle, not a band already saturated to futility; a medium
   that structurally supports revisit; and survivable
   placement, since a drop point that exposes anyone is
   disqualified whatever its coupling.

3. **Feedback must be able to change the work.** What comes
   back from the audience must be able to alter form,
   subjects, and schedule — or the method is transmission with
   better manners. Working with, not for, is the two-way
   condition stated as design.

## 3. The root

The method is Goethean observation turned outward.

Delicate empiricism — observing a living thing repeatedly over
time, without imposed theory, until the thing shows itself —
is a private scientific discipline. This method performs the
same discipline in public, with an audience watching the
repetition. The revisit pulse is Goethe's repeated
observation; the no-narrator rule is his refusal of imposed
theory; the audience completing the meaning is the phenomenon
showing itself to the observer rather than being explained at
them.

This matters for honesty of lineage: the method is not an
assembly of borrowed parts from the field survey. It is one
older method, redirected — and the borrowed parts (§4) confirm
it from other directions rather than compose it.

* * *

## 4. Lineage — confirmations, not components

Each commitment exists somewhere in the field alone; none
exist together, and none carry the falsifiability requirement:

- Duration: sponsorship and twinning models, where the
  relationship outlasts every campaign around it, and where
  participation itself converts attitudes over years.
- Membership: participatory action research, where subjects
  are co-researchers; the toloka, where belonging follows the
  contribution.
- Demonstration: Fuller — build the new model that makes the
  old obsolete; prefigurative politics generally.
- Falsifiability: deep canvassing's measurement culture — the
  only advocacy literature that published its own failures and
  got stronger for it.
- Efficacy mechanics: solutions journalism's finding that
  demonstrated agency lowers the cost of looking and reduces
  avoidance — the theory of change, with evidence.

The combination is the contribution. The parts are not claimed
as new.

## 5. Design constraints that follow

Stated generally; each travels with the method into any
instance:

- **Capture design.** Every output is built to survive hostile
  re-use; when in doubt, it stays unpublished. Reach
  multiplies exposure, so reach is a design input, not a
  distribution afterthought.
- **The vulnerable are not material.** Classes of subjects
  whose exposure cannot be made safe are excluded by design,
  and no gate opens the exclusion. In wartime this includes
  children, precise places, and schedules tied to named
  people.
- **Proportionate traces.** No act of participation carries a
  trace more legible or more durable than the act itself
  requires; every step up the gradient is the participant's
  own named step. Cryptography may touch artifacts, never
  people.
- **No fabricated subjects.** No composites, no invented
  detail, no placeholder presented as real; scaffolding is
  always flagged as scaffolding. An empty region is the
  correct answer until real consented material exists.

## 6. The instantiation test

Something is an instance of the method only if all five hold:

1. A real, revisitable subject — something that will still be
   there, changed, when the observer returns.
2. An owner-member, not a source — the shown person holds the
   showing.
3. A form with no narrator — the audience completes the
   meaning.
4. A stated failure condition — the instance says in advance
   what would show it is not working.
5. A named blocking belief — the audience's actual obstacle,
   diagnosed, not assumed.

**A worked example — a living map of places where something is
growing:** the point is the revisitable subject; membership
follows the point; the sixty-second no-narrator story is the
form; a room exercise carries the predicted near-null and the
hostile-read round; futility — not opposition — is the named
blocking belief. Five for five, which is what would make such
a map a designed instance rather than an application.

## 7. What the method declines

Declined by name, with reasons — not merely omitted:

- **The rebuttal mode.** Correction repeats the frame it
  corrects; the correction literature backs the refusal.
- **The manifesto mode.** Declared identity invites contest;
  demonstrated existence does not argue.
- **Fictional carriers.** Entertainment-education works, and
  is refused anyway: the method's authority rests entirely on
  nothing being invented. Serialisation transfers; fiction
  does not.
- **The urgency register.** Urgency is episodic by nature and
  spends trust the durational form needs. The method loses
  every news cycle by design and accepts this.
- **Aggregate spectacle.** As instances multiply, the single
  subject must remain the unit of encounter — compassion
  collapses at scale, and a map that becomes a statistic has
  left the method.

## 8. Honest limits

- **First designed instance, not first instance.** The
  durational-membered-demonstrative combination emerged
  independently on at least four continents — the
  Stolpersteine, the Green Belt Movement, the Madres de Plaza
  de Mayo, the Treatment Action Campaign, the AIDS Memorial
  Quilt — almost always in movements too poor to buy reach.
  That convergent evolution is stronger evidence of generality
  than any single designed instance could be. What remains a
  hypothesis is narrower, and stated plainly: whether
  deliberate design can reproduce what constraint discovered,
  and whether the fourth commitment — falsifiability, which
  none of the converged cases carried by intent — adds what
  this method claims it adds. A first deliberately designed
  instance is the test of both. And the convergence is among
  winners, not a guarantee: Esperanto ran more than a century
  on three commitments and never tipped. The full record is
  held in the advocacy methods reference published alongside
  this document.
- **Attribution is unsolved.** The method cannot prove the
  crack it causes — a weakness shared with the entire field,
  named rather than hidden. The falsifiability commitment
  bounds the claim; it does not close the gap.
- **Slow by design.** Durational evidence cannot answer an
  episodic emergency. The method is wrong for crisis response
  and does not pretend otherwise.
- **Scale is bounded by consent.** Membership architecture
  does not compress. Growth is limited by the pace of real
  consent conversations, and any shortcut breaks the second
  commitment. This limit is accepted as a feature — it is also
  what makes the trust legible.

* * *

## 9. Open questions

- **The name.** Unnamed by design; "trimtab method" is a
  placeholder that names the mechanism, not the thing. The
  lived concepts of mutual work — toloka, dugnad, meitheal,
  harambee — suggest the name may already exist somewhere
  rather than needing coinage.

- **A second designed instance.** Convergence supplies
  historical instances; deliberate design has yet to prove
  one. Which domain tests reproduction first belongs to
  whoever picks the method up.

- **The measurement.** The instruments are stated in the
  advocacy reference, §7; no instance has yet run them from a
  T-zero baseline to a published null. Until one has, the
  fourth commitment is a design, not a result.
